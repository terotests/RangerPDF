# PDF viewer parity with pdf.js

GENERATED by `web/pdf/tools/bench.js`. Corpus: pdf.js test/pdfs @ cfab9b7 (the files in its test manifest that are in the repository, 781); reference: pdfjs-dist 4.10.38. The viewer runs as its es6 build. Per file: page 1 is rendered at 72 dpi on white by both and compared pixel by pixel (a pixel matches when no channel differs by more than 40); the text of the first 5 pages is compared without white space. Per-file numbers: `parity/results.tsv`.

Not implemented, and so behind most of the differences: JPEG 2000, JBIG2 and CCITT image decoding (those images are drawn grey; a JPEG that does not decode is too), mesh shadings, blend modes and soft masks, predefined CJK CMaps, annotations without an appearance stream. Glyphs are anti-aliased with 4x4 samples, pdf.js uses the browser canvas, so edges of text differ slightly everywhere.
